# Script and Thinking: Stay in the Driver's Seat

## How I decided what goes where
- Your outline says each topic needs the repository's key points, one mainstream example, and one example from your field.
- Your repository page is "Start Here," so I built this deck around that page and kept the template's design.
- Mainstream example: cooking, because anyone can follow it. Field example: a Finance & Accounting variance review. Both use the same five steps so the audience sees the ideas transfer.
- The finance example includes "leave out client names and account numbers" to preview the Ethics, Privacy & Security slide.

## Slide-by-slide script (about 1.5 to 2 minutes each)

**1. Title.** "Today is about one idea: AI can help you think, but you stay in charge."

**2. Why does this matter?** (template slide kept) "AI is becoming part of how we learn and work. The question is whether we use it on purpose or just accept what it gives us."

**3. Five Step Loop.** "AI is an assistant, not an authority. Define the goal, give context, explore, check, decide. The two steps people skip are Check and Decide, and they matter most."

**4. Cooking example.** "Say I want dinner for four in 30 minutes on $20. I tell AI what is in my fridge, ask for three options, check safe temperatures and allergens, then choose and cook. AI never tasted anything. I did."

**5. Finance & Accounting example.** "Travel expense is over budget before close. I give AI an anonymized report, not client data. It suggests causes: timing, miscoding, duplicates. Then I trace each one to the ledger and receipts and recompute the totals. I decide what to adjust or escalate, and I document why. Confidence is not proof."

**6. Recap.** "Assistant, not authority. Five steps. You set the goal, you decide."

**7. Thank you.** Invite questions or ask the audience for a goal from their own major.
