# Script and Thinking: Stay in the Driver's Seat

## How I decided what goes where
- Your outline asks for the repository's key points, one example anyone can follow, and one from your field.
- You asked for simpler examples and for accounting and finance to be separate, so the field part is now two slides.
- Every example is something a student has done: cooking, checking a bank balance, saving for a trip.
- Each example uses the same five steps, so the audience sees the idea transfer.
- Other majors (computer science, engineering, and more) are in the examples folder, not the deck, to keep the talk short.

## Slide-by-slide script (about 1.5 to 2 minutes each)

**1. Title.** "One idea today: AI can help you think, but you stay in charge."

**2. Why does this matter?** "AI is part of how we learn and work. The question is whether we use it on purpose or just accept what it gives us."

**3. Five Step Loop.** "AI is an assistant, not an authority. Define the goal, give context, explore, check, decide. People skip Check and Decide, and those matter most."

**4. Cooking.** "I want dinner for four, 30 minutes, $20. I tell AI what is in my kitchen, ask for three ideas, check safe temperatures, then I choose and cook."

**5. Accounting.** "My bank balance is $40 lower than my notes. I give AI my purchase list, with no account numbers. It suggests a pending charge, a subscription, or a duplicate. I match each one to the statement myself. The statement is the evidence, not AI."

**6. Finance.** "I want $600 for spring break in 4 months. AI lays out three plans. I do the math: $150 a month. I choose, because AI doesn't know my priorities. This is not financial advice."

**7. Recap.** "Assistant, not authority. Five steps. You set the goal, you decide."

**8. Thank you.** Ask the audience for a goal from their own major.
