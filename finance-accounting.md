# Finance & Accounting: Stay in the Driver's Seat

[Back to Start Here](../README.md)

**Remember:** You set the goal. AI assists. You decide.

## The goal
Explain why Q3 travel expense is over budget before the monthly close.

## The five-step loop
1. **Define the goal:** Explain why Q3 travel expense is over budget before the monthly close.
2. **Give context:** Share an anonymized variance report, the travel policy, and the period. Leave out client names, employee names, and account numbers.
3. **Explore:** Ask AI for possible causes (timing, miscoding, duplicate entries, one-time events) and for a way to organize the investigation.
4. **Check:** Trace every lead to the general ledger and receipts. Recompute totals yourself. Confirm any accounting rule cited against the actual standard.
5. **Decide:** You decide what to adjust, accrue, or escalate, and you document the reasoning for the reviewer.
